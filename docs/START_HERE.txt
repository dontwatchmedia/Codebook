CODEBOOK 0.1 — EARLY EDITION

Extract the ZIP, then double-click CodeBook.exe.

No account, internet connection, Node.js, or Rust is needed to write.
The app uses the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime included on this machine.

Open the Learning C++ sample, or choose New book to start writing.

Your books are saved in your Windows app-data folder, not beside the EXE.
The Preferences screen shows the exact location. Moving the EXE does not
move your library. Use Export > CodeBook project to back up or transfer
a complete book, including notes and images.

This release implements the core writing MVP. PDF, EPUB, DOCX, AI, sync,
and collaboration are not included. See README.md for the feature list
and limitations, and VALIDATION.md for testing evidence.

The executable is unsigned.
